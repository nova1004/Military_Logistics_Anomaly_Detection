How to read these results

  • Anomaly: a shipment whose numbers look very different from most other shipments in this dataset.
  • Anomaly score: lower (more negative) means more unusual. Negative scores are the ones the model flagged.
  • Not a verdict: a flag only suggests a record for human review. It does not say why the record is unusual or that anything harmful happened.

Evaluation note

Isolation Forest is unsupervised and this dataset has no genuine labels, so no accuracy, precision or recall is reported here. The contamination setting only controls roughly how many records get flagged; it is not a real-world anomaly rate.
